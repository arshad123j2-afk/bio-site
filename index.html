About

I'm a first-year student from Nagercoil, still figuring out exactly where my interests will take me, but enjoying the process. Outside of coursework, most of my week revolves around three things: football, the gym, and a book I'm slowly making my way through.

Football is where I unwind — there's something about a game that clears my head better than anything else. The gym is more of a discipline than a hobby at this point; I like the quiet routine of showing up and doing the work, even on days I don't feel like it. And reading is the constant — fiction mostly, though I'll pick up almost anything if the first few pages hook me. Somewhere between these three, I'm learning a lot about consistency, patience, and what it means to actually stick with something.
